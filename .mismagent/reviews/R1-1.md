## Dimensions below 4 and the three worst places

1. **Error handling: `ui/src/main/kotlin/snastro/ui/riassunto/RiassuntoPresenter.kt:177-180` and `:351`, with `impostazioni/LunghezzaRiassuntoPresenter.kt:69`.**
   - **The defect:** R1's own screen.
     - A throwing read kills the tab's only reload collector, silently.
     - The lunghezza massima save is unguarded on S3 but `nonFataleRegistrato` in Impostazioni, for one committing command.
   - **The fix:** route `ricarica`'s reads through `nonFataleRegistrato` and show M1(b)'s message when they fail; this keeps the collector alive. Route both `modificaLunghezzaMassima` edges through `eccezioneRegistrata`. Ideally the S3 tab reuses one editor with Impostazioni.
   - **Tests:** a throwing `vista`, and a throwing `modificaLunghezzaMassimaCmd`, in `RiassuntoPresenterGuastiTest`.
   - No new decision is needed: this is ADR 0041 §3 as written.
2. **Error handling: `trascrizione/applicazione/src/main/kotlin/snastro/trascrizione/applicazione/comandi/EseguiProssimaElaborazioneServizio.kt:383-401`.**
   - **The defect:** every phase fault of a transcription is reduced to `null` with no trace.
   - **The fix:** hand the cause to a `Segnalazione` (`:supporto`) passed in by `:avvio`, as `CodaCondivisa` does with `segnalaSfuggito`. The KDoc's "no logging sink" premise is out of date.
   - This changes the service's collaborators, so it is the architect's call whether it is a port or a constructor parameter.
3. **Simple design: S3 · `ui/src/main/kotlin/snastro/ui/registrazione/StatoVoci.kt` (728), `SchermataPannelloVoci.kt` (899), `SchermataRegistrazione.kt` (798, `LongMethod` at `:334`), `RegistrazionePresenter.kt:71`.**
   - **The defect:** S2 has been split into flows (slices 33–35), but S3 has not. R0-3 already named it.
   - **The fix:** apply presenter-split and screen-split to S3 the way slices 34–35 applied them to S2. The flows are the Voci panel's card commands, the Revisione, naming a phrase, and the proposals/pair between Parti. One bundle each, built by `:avvio`, so that the suppressions drop.
