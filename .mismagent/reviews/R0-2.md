## Dimensions below 4 and the three worst places

1. **Simple design: `ui/src/main/kotlin/snastro/ui/registrazioni/RegistrazioniPresenter.kt:98`**
   - The class has 21 constructor parameters and `TooManyFunctions` suppressed. Its companion `SchermataRegistrazioni.kt:3` is 1503 lines under a file-level `TooManyFunctions` suppression.
   - Slice 16 moved the import and row-delete flows out, but the row commands (`suRiga`, `annullaElaborazione`, `modificaOraDiInizio`, ...), the Incontro commands (`avviaElaborazioniIncontro`, `aggiornaIncontro`, `incontroLibero`) and playback (`riproduci`, `pausa`) still share the class.
   - Fix: continue presenter-split (S2) with one or two more holders over `StatoS2`, for example an Incontro flow and a row-commands flow. Each takes only its own collaborators, so the constructor's lambdas spread out.
   - Fix for the view: split it along the same flows (already a design note).

2. **Duplication and error handling: the S2 row-command handler, `RegistrazioniPresenter.kt:541-571`, `:637-667` and `FlussoEliminaRiga.kt:59-88`**
   - The same guard/launch/catch/finally is written three times. Every copy catches `Exception` and drops the cause unlogged (CR-7).
   - Fix: one row runner on `StatoS2`. It owns the row guard, the in-flight flag and the reset in `finally`, and maps a fault to the generic `erroreRiga` with a log line. The three callers pass only their success and refusal handling.
   - That runner is also where the logging decision already recorded in design notes would live: an `Exception`-only variant of the helper, so `Error`s keep propagating as D-0065/L269 require. This is an architect's call; it touches CR-19b and non-fatal-faults.

3. **Error handling: causes dropped at presenter edges, including R0's own S1 screen**
   - `ui/src/main/kotlin/snastro/ui/progetti/ProgettiPresenter.kt:56`: a failed project-list load is caught as `Exception` with `SwallowedException` and shows only the generic `erroreElenco`. A corrupt or unreadable registry would leave no trace in the log.
   - `RegistrazioniPresenter.kt:607`: `avviaElaborazioniIncontro` does `catturaNonFatale { ... }.getOrNull()` and shows `MESSAGGIO_ERRORE_GENERICO` without logging. This is exactly the shape non-fatal-faults forbids, and `nonFataleRegistrato` is a drop-in replacement there.
   - Other degraded reads without a log: `RegistrazioniPresenter.kt:279,336`, `registrazione/StatoVoci.kt:232,477,484`, `registrazione/RegistrazionePresenter.kt:215-216,249`, `riassunto/RiassuntoPresenter.kt:403`. These were already in design notes.
   - Fix: move `ProgettiPresenter.kt:56` and `RegistrazioniPresenter.kt:607` onto `nonFataleRegistrato`, or log inside the catch. Then move the degraded reads, one slice per screen.
