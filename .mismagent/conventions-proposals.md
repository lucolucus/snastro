# Conventions proposals
- create test-helpers: a test helper used by two or more modules lives once in `:supporto-test` (`snastro.supporto.test`, public, JDK/test-library only, no snastro dependency); a module keeps at most a thin delegating wrapper for its own defaults. First case: the log spy `registriDi(logger, blocco)` — `supporto-test/src/main/kotlin/snastro/supporto/test/RegistriDi.kt`, `ui/src/test/kotlin/snastro/ui/LogUi.kt` (`registriLogUi` delegates), `avvio/src/test/kotlin/snastro/avvio/PreferenzeAppFileTest.kt` (slice 43 review B1: the spy was copied verbatim across modules; the caller holds the logger strongly in a file-level `val`, since `LogManager` keeps named loggers weakly)
